COMMON QUESTIONS

What is a snapshot?

It is a record of eligible NFT ownership at a specified point in time or blockchain block. Buying a Ticket after the snapshot does not automatically qualify you for that snapshot's benefits.

How many next-prefecture Tickets can I buy in the priority sale?

You can buy up to one next-prefecture Journey Ticket for each eligible Artwork NFT held from the previous prefecture, but no more than 3 Tickets per wallet. Holding 3 or more Artworks allows up to 3 Tickets. Holding 10 Artworks does not raise the limit.

Do I need the previous prefecture's Artwork to join?

Not necessarily. Previous Artwork ownership provides priority access to the next Ticket. Public sales, when offered, let newcomers join too.

What if I own several Tickets?

Each eligible Ticket allows one free Artwork claim and up to two special-price Artwork purchases in separate stages. For example, 3 eligible Tickets allow up to 3 free Artworks and up to 6 special-price purchases, subject to supply and sale conditions.

Does a Ticket guarantee Artwork?

No. Eligibility, stage limits, available supply, transaction success and official sale terms all apply.

Where are the exact dates and prices?

Check the official website, the project's X account and the linked OpenSea sale pages. Always verify links before connecting a wallet.
