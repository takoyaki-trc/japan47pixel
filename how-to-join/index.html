UNDERSTANDING THE TWO ARTWORK STAGES

FREE CLAIM

1 ART / TICKET

An eligible holder can claim up to one Artwork NFT for each Ticket counted at the snapshot. The Artwork price is zero; network fees may still apply.

PAID HOLDER PRESALE

UP TO 2 / TICKET

A separate paid stage for eligible Ticket holders, with a planned limit of up to two Artwork NFTs per Ticket. The price is announced for each chapter.

These are stage-specific limits, not a promise of available supply. Any treatment of unused free claims in later stages will be specified in the official sale terms; do not assume automatic carryover.

COMMON QUESTIONS

What is a snapshot?

It is a record of eligible NFT ownership at a specified point in time or blockchain block. Buying a Ticket after the snapshot does not automatically qualify you for that snapshot’s benefits.

Do I need the previous prefecture’s Artwork to join?

Not necessarily. Previous Artwork ownership is intended to provide priority access to the next Ticket. Public sales, when offered, let newcomers join too.

What if I own several Tickets?

The planned Artwork limits are calculated per eligible Ticket: one free claim and up to two paid presale purchases per Ticket, subject to each stage’s final rules and supply.

Does a Ticket guarantee Artwork?

No. Eligibility, stage limits, available supply, transaction success and official sale terms all apply.

Where are the exact dates and prices?

Check the official website, the project’s X account and the linked OpenSea sale pages. Always verify links before connecting a wallet.
