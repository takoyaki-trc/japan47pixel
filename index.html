QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

What is TRAVELING TAKOYAKI?

TRAVELING TAKOYAKI — Japan 47 Journey is a pixel-art NFT project that follows a takoyaki truck across all 47 prefectures of Japan. Each destination has its own Journey Ticket and NFT art chapter.

What is a Journey Ticket?

A Journey Ticket is a collectible NFT associated with a specific prefecture. Ticket holders may receive access to announced benefits, including eligibility for priority purchase opportunities, subject to the conditions of each chapter.

Are Journey Tickets free?

Ticket prices may vary by destination. Some releases may be free to mint, while network fees may still apply. Always check the official release details.

Does a Journey Ticket guarantee an NFT artwork?

No. A Journey Ticket does not guarantee an artwork or a successful purchase. Eligibility, snapshots, wallet limits and supply are determined by each chapter's announced rules.

When will new destinations and NFTs be released?

Release schedules will be announced through official social media channels and OpenSea. Please check the latest announcements for confirmed dates.

What are the five rarity tiers?

The five tiers are Normal (N), Rare (R), Super Rare (SR), Ultra Rare (UR), and Legendary (LR). Any percentages shown as examples are illustrative and are not confirmed allocations for future chapters.

Where can I find the official OpenSea collection?

Official collection links are provided in the Ticket Book and NFT Art Book when available. Please verify the destination before connecting your wallet.

Can I join the journey later?

Yes. You can follow the project at any stage. Availability of past Tickets and NFT artworks depends on their release status and secondary-market listings.

How can I stay safe?

Always verify official links. Never share your wallet's seed phrase or private keys. Be cautious of impersonators and unsolicited direct messages.
