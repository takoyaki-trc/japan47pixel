QUESTIONS BEFORE DEPARTURE

FAQ & SAFETY

What is TRAVELING TAKOYAKI?

A pixel-art NFT journey across all 47 prefectures of Japan. Each stop has a Journey Ticket and a separately released artwork NFT collection.

What is a Journey Ticket?

A prefecture-specific NFT. Holding an eligible ticket at an announced snapshot may provide priority access to purchase artwork NFTs for that prefecture.

Are all Journey Tickets free?

Hokkaido's ticket was free to mint, apart from possible network fees. Future destinations may have different terms and prices.

Does a ticket guarantee an artwork NFT?

No. Art NFTs are purchased separately and are subject to eligibility, supply, availability, and per-wallet limits.

When is the Hokkaido art mint?

The artwork sale date, price, final supply and ticket-holder snapshot date have not yet been announced.

What are the five rarities?

N, R, SR, UR and LR. Each has its own frame and time-of-day background atmosphere. The example distribution is not the final mint distribution.

Where are the official OpenSea pages?

Select a prefecture in the Ticket Book or NFT Art Book. Each collection has its own OpenSea URL when released.

Can I join at a later stop?

Yes. Future ticket eligibility and release conditions will be announced for each stop.

STAY SAFE

Never share your seed phrase or private key. Check URLs carefully and use only official project links.
