FAQ & SAFETY

What is TRAVELING TAKOYAKI?

TRAVELING TAKOYAKI is a pixel-art NFT journey across all 47 prefectures of Japan. Each destination has its own Journey Ticket and artwork NFT collection.

What is a Journey Ticket?

A Journey Ticket is a prefecture-specific NFT. Eligible holders at an announced snapshot may receive priority access to purchase artwork NFTs for that destination.

Are Journey Tickets always free?

Not necessarily. Ticket prices, supply and distribution conditions may vary by destination. Please check the official announcements for each stop. Network fees may also apply.

Does a Journey Ticket guarantee an artwork NFT?

No. A Journey Ticket does not guarantee an artwork NFT. Artwork NFTs are purchased separately and may be subject to eligibility requirements, limited supply, availability and per-wallet limits.

How can I find upcoming NFT releases?

Release dates, snapshot schedules, prices and other important updates will be announced through our official social media accounts and OpenSea collections. Check the latest announcements before participating.

What are the five rarities?

The five rarity levels are N (Normal), R (Rare), SR (Super Rare), UR (Ultra Rare) and LR (Legend Rare). Rarity distributions and artwork details are announced for each collection.

Where can I find the official OpenSea collections?

Visit the Journey Ticket Book or NFT Art Collection Book on this website. Each released destination has its own official OpenSea collection link.

Can I join the journey at a later stop?

Yes! You can join the journey at any destination. Each stop has its own release schedule and participation conditions, so you do not need to have collected previous tickets to follow the journey.

STAY SAFE

Never share your seed phrase or private key. Always verify official URLs before connecting your wallet or making a purchase.
