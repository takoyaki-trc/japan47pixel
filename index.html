QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

What is TRAVELING TAKOYAKI?

TRAVELING TAKOYAKI — Japan 47 Journey is a pixel-art NFT project that follows a takoyaki truck across all 47 prefectures of Japan.

What is a Journey Ticket?

A Journey Ticket is a collectible NFT associated with a specific prefecture.

Are Journey Tickets free?

Ticket prices may vary by destination. Some releases may be free to mint, while network fees may still apply.

Does a Journey Ticket guarantee an NFT artwork?

No. A Journey Ticket does not guarantee an artwork or a successful purchase.

When will new destinations and NFTs be released?

Release schedules will be announced through official social media channels and OpenSea.

What are the five rarity tiers?

The five tiers are Normal, Rare, Super Rare, Ultra Rare, and Legendary.

Where can I find the official OpenSea collection?

Use the official OpenSea link at the bottom of this website.

Can I join the journey later?

Yes. You can follow the project at any stage.

How can I stay safe?

Always verify official links. Never share your wallet's seed phrase or private keys.
