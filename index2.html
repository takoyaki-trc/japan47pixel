QUESTIONS BEFORE DEPARTURE

FAQ

What is Traveling Takoyaki?

Traveling Takoyaki is a pixel-art NFT journey across all 47 prefectures of Japan. Each destination becomes a new chapter inspired by its landscapes, food and local stories.

What is a Journey Ticket?

A Journey Ticket is a free NFT issued before each prefecture's main collection. It acts as your boarding pass for that destination and gives its holder priority access to the destination NFT mint.

Is the Journey Ticket free?

Yes. The Journey Ticket itself is a free mint. A network transaction fee may still apply depending on the blockchain and wallet. Final details will be announced before minting begins.

Does a ticket guarantee an NFT?

No. A ticket gives you priority access, but it does not guarantee a purchase. The destination NFT is minted separately and availability is limited.

How is the NFT supply decided?

The number of genuine Journey Ticket holders helps determine the supply for that prefecture. Each chapter will have a minimum of 99 NFTs and a planned maximum of 999. The final number will be announced before the main mint.

How much will a destination NFT cost?

The planned mint price is approximately US$3 per NFT, excluding network fees. The confirmed price will be published on the official website before each drop.

Can I join the journey at a later stop?

Yes. You can join at any prefecture. You do not need to own an earlier Journey Ticket or NFT, although collecting every stop creates a complete record of the journey.

What are the five rarities?

The collection uses Normal, Rare, Super Rare, Ultra Rare and Legendary Rare frames. Each prefecture includes only one Legendary Rare NFT, issued as a unique 1/1 artwork.

When will the next prefecture be released?

The journey aims to reach a new destination approximately every six to eight weeks. The schedule may change to protect the quality of each chapter.

How is the artwork created?

The concept, art direction, composition and pixel editing are created by JAPAN47PIXEL. AI may be used as a production aid, followed by manual selection, correction and pixel-level refinement.
